A MINUTE TO LEARN

Simple rules.
Endless “one more?”

  1. Choose a private room, share a device, or challenge Panda Bot. Ink starts the first round.
  2. Take turns placing your panda on an empty intersection.
  3. Connect five horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.

On a phone, tap to preview, then tap the same spot to place. On a keyboard, use arrow keys and Enter.
